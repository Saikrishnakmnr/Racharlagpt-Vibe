Only merchants who explicitly requested removal, passed the 60-day expiry grace and seven-day cancellation window, and have not renewed are eligible. Their billing and customer-order history remains untouched.

Verify your administrator token first. No cleanup runs automatically.

Caution: A completed cleanup removes the old hosted catalog and its recorded image files. This cannot be undone. Never clean a merchant who renewed or has an earlier protected license.

What changes automatically?

Sales page, owner monthly activation, future checkout orders and payment receipt validation follow the saved monthly amount. New purchase orders preserve their price even if the admin changes the rate afterward. No edit to the admin panel can bypass the separate payment-launch safety switch.
