Privacy policy

Effective 6 November 2026 · Applies to the ₹99/month Racharla Business Agent hosted shop service. Earlier Vibe product terms remain available on the legacy terms page.

1. Merchant account and shop details

We process sign-in identifiers, business names and addresses, WhatsApp contact numbers, shop descriptions, products, catalog photos and merchant payment instructions that you choose to publish. Your public shop page intentionally displays the business information you provide, including the business WhatsApp number and address.

2. Customer order information

When customers create an order request, the hosted service stores an order reference, selected products, estimated prices, customer name and phone number, delivery address where supplied, payment preference, note, order status and timestamps. These records are intended to be visible only to the relevant merchant and authorized service administrators, not to unrelated sellers. Order requests are also shared with the merchant through WhatsApp after the customer manually sends the drafted message.

3. Payment data

Razorpay processes the ₹99 hosted-service payment; we may keep order/payment identifiers, amounts, status, renewal periods and support records. Business Agent does not require merchants to provide their bank login details or store customers' card numbers. Merchants enter their own public UPI IDs or upload payment QR codes; we do not confirm whether an individual customer has paid the merchant.

4. Hosting and service providers

Account and catalog data use Supabase services, with hosting, connectivity, security and payments supported by other third-party service providers as configured by the service. Browser-local data may also be retained on the device for draft editing, so use a trusted device and sign out before sharing it.

5. Retention, deletion and security

Catalog data is retained after subscription expiry to make restarting easier, subject to operational, legal and security requirements. Order and billing records may be retained for business operations, support and legally required accounting. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion where permitted by applicable law. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Customer information

Shop owners are responsible for handling customer information received in WhatsApp or outside the service, using it only for legitimate order-related purposes and respecting customer preferences and applicable law. We do not provide built-in bulk WhatsApp marketing.

For service questions or billing issues, please use our contact page.
