Service terms

Effective 6 November 2026 · Applies to the ₹99/month Racharla Business Agent hosted shop service. Earlier Vibe product terms remain available on the legacy terms page.

1. The service

Business Agent lets merchants create a hosted business page, edit catalog items and prices, publish a shareable URL, receive customer order requests through WhatsApp and manage order statuses. The merchant is responsible for the products, price accuracy, customer communications and order fulfillment. This is not a managed delivery or merchant-payment-processing service.

2. Monthly price and renewal

Hosted access is offered at ₹99 for one prepaid monthly period. Each completed payment adds one calendar month of access, starting immediately if there is no active period, or extending the existing paid-through date if access is already active. There is no automatic recurring debit on this prepaid plan. You may stop simply by not purchasing the next month. If you renew within the applicable retention grace period (currently 60 days after the last paid period), sign in to the same account and continue with the saved catalog. Renewal after that grace period may require creating a fresh catalog.

3. Expiry and data

When a paid period ends, the public ordering page pauses and publishing changes is disabled until renewal. Catalog information is eligible for reactivation during a 60-day grace period after expiry, subject to changes to this policy with appropriate advance notice. After the grace period, a new catalog may be required. Download a backup before resetting; stale online media and catalogs may later be removed under a separately announced retention process. Essential payment records may be retained for legal obligations. No unlimited or perpetual free storage is promised. To request account or data deletion, contact support. Access can be suspended for abuse, fraud or unlawful content.

4. Customer orders and merchant payments

Customers submit an order request; the app generates an order reference and opens a WhatsApp draft, but the customer must tap Send. The shop owner must accept the order, confirm availability, taxes, delivery fees, final prices and timing. Customer UPI and QR payments are made to the shop owner's own account; Business Agent does not verify their receipt and does not automatically capture these customer payments. Merchant responsibilities, cancellations, refunds and complaints relating to goods or services remain with that merchant and applicable consumer-protection law.

5. Product content and limits

Owners must use content, photographs, names, prices and product information they have rights to publish. The initial hosted service supports one shop catalog per account and up to 200 items. Photos are compressed, and reasonable bandwidth, upload and anti-abuse limits may apply to keep the ₹99 plan affordable. We may revise limits or pricing for future billing periods with notice as required by applicable law; an already-paid period is not retroactively repriced.

6. Billing disputes and refunds

Razorpay handles Business Agent purchase payments. If a payment is captured but your access is not activated, use Recover Payment in the dashboard and then contact support with your transaction ID. Billing errors and refund requests will be evaluated in accordance with applicable law, payment-provider requirements and the circumstances of the request. Refunds are not controlled by shop owners for their separate customer payments.

7. Availability

Internet access and our hosting provider are needed for live catalogs, shared shop links and customer ordering. No particular uptime, number of orders, business growth or earnings is guaranteed. The platform does not send WhatsApp messages automatically or synchronize offline HTML snapshots to online shops.

8. Contact

Use the linked contact page for operational support and the applicable privacy policy for personal-data requests. Merchants should provide their own customer contact and trading information.

For service questions or billing issues, please use our contact page.
