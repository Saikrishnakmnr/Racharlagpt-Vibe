Frequently asked questions

Is ₹99 a lifetime fee?

No. ₹99 covers one prepaid month. Renew to continue hosting and using your published shop.

What happens if I stop paying?

The hosted public shop pauses when access expires. Your saved data remains tied to your account for reactivation, subject to the published retention terms.

Do customers pay Racharla?

No. They choose the shop's payment preferences. The shop owner verifies payment directly.

Does WhatsApp automatically send orders?

No. It opens a prepared order message and the customer taps Send.

Can I edit prices from my phone?

Yes. Open the hosted owner dashboard, edit products and publish updates. Your customers can refresh the latest catalog online.
