How to publish, share and keep customer apps updated

1. Sign in and activate your Business Agent license.
2. Add your products, photos, prices and WhatsApp number.
3. Choose a public shop address and select Publish shop.
4. Click Save & publish, then copy the shop URL.
5. Share it through WhatsApp or place it in your Instagram bio or story link.
6. Edit and publish again when stock, prices or offers change. Customers see your latest hosted catalog after refresh.
