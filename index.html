What if I don't want to continue?

Can I stop after one month?

Yes. Billing is prepaid and manual. We don't automatically collect next month's payment.

Will my shop details disappear?

No. Your business details and catalog remain saved for reactivation. Public ordering pauses after the paid period expires.

What happens when I renew later?

Sign into the same account, pay the current displayed monthly rate for another month and publish your existing shop again. You do not need to recreate the catalog.

Who receives my customers' money?

Your customers pay you directly using your own UPI, merchant QR or enabled cash option. Racharla does not process these shop payments.

Are WhatsApp orders automatically sent or paid?

No. Customers must press Send in WhatsApp. You confirm the order and any UPI or cash payment yourself.

Give your shop a professional website and one link you can share with everyone. Keep your catalog updated from the owner dashboard.
